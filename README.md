### Story-Viewer

Easily play, share, and download WhatsApp statuses — keep your favorite stories saved forever, even
after 24 hours!

###### Features

- All Statuses in One Place: Quickly browse through all WhatsApp statuses without switching apps
- Seamless Media Playback: View images and play video statuses effortlessly
- Permanent Downloads: Save statuses to your device for unlimited access anytime
- Easy Sharing: Share statuses directly with your friends in just a few taps
